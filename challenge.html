ONCE WARRIOR,ALWAYS A WARRIOR

The samurai were warriors shaped by discipline ,honor and courage.They adhered to principles of Bushido,approaching every challenge with composure and unwavering resolve. To a samurai, the sword represented more than a weapon: it symbolized resposiblity ,loyalty, and determenation.Whether facin victory or defeat, they maintained their diginty and refused to be governed by fear. A true warrior may set aside his sword,but the warrior within never truly fades.

Their leagacy reminds us that true strength is not measured onlt by physical power,but by the courage to stand firm ,the wisdom to act with restraint, and the resolve to remain qaitful to one's principles
